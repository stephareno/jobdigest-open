# FP&A Director
Company: Los Pollos Hermanos
Location: Albuquerque, NM
Work model: In-office
Pay: $140,000 - $155,000 a year

Los Pollos Hermanos is a fast-casual restaurant chain with 140 locations across the Southwest, known for fresh chicken, house-made sides and fast service. We are a 4,000-person company, and we are adding twenty restaurants a year.

We are hiring an FP&A Director to lead planning and analysis from our Albuquerque headquarters. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and lead a team of two analysts.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual budget and the monthly forecast for the company and each restaurant.
- Build the long-range plan and the new-restaurant investment model.
- Track new-restaurant openings against their investment cases.
- Analyze restaurant-level margins, labor and food costs with operations leaders.
- Support pricing and menu decisions with clear analysis.
- Lead and develop the FP&A team.

Qualifications:
- 8+ years in FP&A, including several in restaurants, retail or another multi-unit business.
- Experience leading one or two analysts.
- Strong command of unit economics and four-wall profitability.
- Comfort working side by side with restaurant operators.

This role is onsite, five days a week at our Albuquerque headquarters. The base salary range is $140,000 to $155,000, plus an annual bonus.

Los Pollos Hermanos is an equal opportunity employer.
