# Director of Finance Systems
Company: Stark Industries
Location: Chicago, IL
Work model: Hybrid
Pay: $165,000 - $185,000 a year

Stark Industries designs and manufactures industrial equipment, power systems and precision components for customers in energy, transportation and construction. We are an 11,000-person company with operations in nine countries.

We are hiring a Director of Finance Systems to lead the technology behind our finance organization. You will report to the Vice President of Finance Transformation and lead a team of six systems analysts and developers.

What you will do:
- Lead the implementation of our new cloud ERP general ledger, accounts payable and fixed assets modules.
- Own the roadmap for finance applications: the ERP, the consolidation tool, the planning tool used by FP&A, and expense management.
- Run integrations between the ERP and our manufacturing, procurement and payroll systems.
- Manage system administration, access controls and audit support for finance applications.
- Lead release management, testing and user training during each rollout.
- Manage the systems integrator and software vendors.

What you bring:
- 10+ years in finance systems, ERP implementation or finance technology, with team leadership.
- Hands-on experience implementing a major ERP and a consolidation or planning tool.
- Understanding of accounting processes and controls.
- You will work closely with FP&A and the controller's team, but this role does not own planning, forecasting or reporting.

How we work: we invest in the people who build our products, and we expect leaders to be candid about risk.

This role is hybrid, three days a week at our Chicago office. The base salary range is $165,000 to $185,000, plus bonus.

Stark Industries is an equal opportunity employer.
