# Director of FP&A, Retail Banking
Company: Gringotts
Location: London, United Kingdom
Work model: Hybrid

Gringotts is a private bank serving families, businesses and institutions from its headquarters in London. It has kept its customers' assets safe for generations and employs about 3,000 people across the United Kingdom.

We are hiring a Director of FP&A for our retail banking division. You will report to the division's Finance Director and lead a team of six.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual plan and the quarterly forecast for retail banking.
- Model net interest income, fee income and operating costs under different rate scenarios.
- Prepare the division's monthly performance pack and its input to the group board.
- Work with product and branch leaders on pricing and cost decisions.
- Support capital planning and regulatory submissions with the treasury and risk teams.
- Lead and develop the planning team.

Qualifications:
- 10+ years in FP&A in banking or financial services.
- Experience leading a team and presenting to senior committees.
- Knowledge of UK banking regulation and capital requirements.
- A professional accounting qualification.

How we work: we are careful, we keep our promises, and we expect the same of everyone who works here.

The role is hybrid, three days a week at our London office. Applicants must have the right to work in the United Kingdom. Salary is competitive and includes an annual bonus.

Gringotts is an equal opportunity employer.
