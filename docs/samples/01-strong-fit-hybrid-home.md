# Director of FP&A
Company: Wonka Industries
Location: Chicago, IL
Work model: Hybrid
Pay: $175,000 - $195,000 a year

Wonka Industries makes chocolate bars, seasonal candy and baking ingredients sold in grocery, club and convenience stores across North America. We run three factories, a test kitchen and a distribution center, and we are a 2,400-person, family-owned consumer products company with $1.3 billion in annual revenue.

We are hiring a Director of FP&A to lead planning for the whole company. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer, lead a team of seven analysts and managers, and own the numbers our leadership team and board run the business on.

What you will own:
- The annual operating plan, from top-down targets through the bottom-up build with every business unit.
- The monthly rolling forecast and the three-year long-range plan.
- The board reporting package and the monthly executive review: results, variance commentary and the outlook.
- Pricing, promotion and capital investment analysis with the commercial and supply chain teams.
- The planning model and the move from spreadsheets to a planning tool next year.
- Hiring, coaching and growing the FP&A team.

About you:
- 10+ years in FP&A or corporate finance, with at least three leading a team.
- Experience in consumer products, food or another manufacturing business with a cost-of-goods story to tell.
- You can explain a variance to a plant manager and to the board in the same afternoon.
- CPA, CFA or MBA preferred.

How we work: decisions are made in the open, the numbers are shared widely, and we promote from within. Every employee gets a share of the profit-sharing pool.

This role is hybrid, three days a week at our Chicago headquarters in the West Loop. The base salary range is $175,000 to $195,000, plus an annual bonus.

Wonka Industries is an equal opportunity employer.
