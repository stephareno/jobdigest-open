# Director of Budget and Financial Planning
Company: Tatooine Department of Treasury
Location: Mos Eisley, Tatooine (off-planet; hired through the London office)
Work model: In-office

The Tatooine Department of Treasury manages the public finances of a remote desert settlement: water rights, trade levies, port fees and the budget of every public service. The department employs about 400 people and reports to the governing council.

We are hiring a Director of Budget and Financial Planning to lead the annual budget and the long-range financial plan. You will report to the Treasurer and lead a team of six budget analysts.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual budget, from departmental requests through council approval.
- Build the multi-year financial plan, including water infrastructure and port investments.
- Forecast revenue from trade levies and port fees, and explain what moves them.
- Prepare the quarterly financial report to the governing council.
- Work with department heads on spending plans and performance measures.
- Lead and develop the budget team.

Qualifications:
- 10+ years in public budgeting, government finance or FP&A, including team leadership.
- Experience presenting a budget to an elected body.
- Strong forecasting skills and clear writing.
- Willingness to relocate off-planet; the department arranges transport and housing.

How we work: we publish every budget, we answer to the public, and we plan for scarcity.

The role is onsite in Mos Eisley. Hiring is managed through the department's London office. Salary follows the department's published pay scale.

The Tatooine Department of Treasury is an equal opportunity employer.
