# Head of FP&A
Company: Pym Technologies
Location: Seattle, WA
Work model: Hybrid
Pay: $170,000 - $200,000 a year

Pym Technologies develops advanced materials: lightweight alloys, specialty coatings and the processes that make them at scale. Our materials are used by aircraft, medical device and electronics manufacturers. We are a 1,500-person company with a research campus and two production sites, and our revenue has doubled in four years.

We are hiring a Head of FP&A to build planning for a company moving from research-led to production-led growth. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and lead a team of five.

What you will own:
- The annual operating plan and the rolling forecast, from the research portfolio to the factory floor.
- The long-range plan and the capital investment cases for new production capacity.
- Spending plans and cost targets with each research and production leader.
- Product-line profitability and the cost models behind our pricing.
- Building and leading the FP&A team as the company grows.

About you:
- 10+ years in FP&A, with experience leading a team at a manufacturing or technology company.
- Experience planning for a business with long research cycles and heavy capital spending.
- You build models others can read and explain them simply.

How we work: we fund curiosity, we share our results openly inside the company, and we are building for the long term.

The role is hybrid, three days a week at our Seattle campus, and we offer relocation support. The base salary range is $170,000 to $200,000, plus bonus and equity.

Pym Technologies is an equal opportunity employer.
