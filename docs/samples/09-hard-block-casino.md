# Director of FP&A
Company: Canto Bight Casino Group
Location: Las Vegas, NV
Work model: In-office
Pay: $160,000 - $190,000 a year

Canto Bight Casino Group owns and operates four resort casinos with hotels, restaurants and entertainment venues. We employ about 9,000 people and host more than twelve million guests a year.

We are hiring a Director of FP&A to lead planning for the group. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and lead a team of eight analysts and managers.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual budget, the monthly forecast and the five-year plan for the group and each property.
- Analyze gaming, hotel, food and beverage, and entertainment results, and explain what drives them.
- Prepare the monthly management package and the quarterly board presentation.
- Build the investment cases for property renovations and new venues.
- Work with property leaders on labor planning, pricing and cost control.
- Lead and develop the FP&A team.

Qualifications:
- 10+ years in FP&A, including several in hospitality, gaming or another multi-property business.
- Experience leading a team of analysts and managers.
- Strong modeling and presentation skills.
- Ability to obtain the gaming license this role requires.

Our culture: we run around the clock, we reward people who keep their composure under pressure, and we promote from within.

The role is onsite at our Las Vegas headquarters. The base salary range is $160,000 to $190,000, plus an annual bonus.

Canto Bight Casino Group is an equal opportunity employer.
