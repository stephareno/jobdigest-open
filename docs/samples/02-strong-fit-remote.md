# Director of Financial Planning and Analysis
Company: Pied Piper
Location: Remote (US)
Work model: Remote
Pay: $160,000 - $185,000 a year

Pied Piper builds data storage software that companies use to keep large files fast to reach and cheap to store. More than 900 customers run on our platform, including the hospitals that keep patients' imaging records on it and the public libraries that archive their collections with us. We are a 450-person software company with recurring revenue of $140 million, growing about 30 percent a year.

We are looking for a Director of Financial Planning and Analysis to lead planning as we prepare for our next stage of growth. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and lead a team of four.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual operating plan and the quarterly reforecast, working with every department head.
- Build and run the long-range plan and the model behind our fundraising and investor updates.
- Track bookings, recurring revenue, retention and unit economics, and explain what moves them.
- Partner with sales, engineering and customer success on headcount, hiring plans and spend.
- Prepare the monthly management package for the executive team.
- Lead, coach and grow a team of four analysts.

Qualifications:
- 8+ years in FP&A, including several at a subscription software company.
- Experience owning a plan and a forecast end to end and leading a team.
- Strong modeling skills and comfort presenting to executives.
- Experience with a planning tool and a modern data stack is a plus.

Our culture: we write things down, we share context widely, and we give people room to own their work. We publish our salary bands internally.

The role is fully remote within the United States, with a team gathering twice a year. The base salary range is $160,000 to $185,000, plus equity.

Pied Piper is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applicants from every background.
