# Director of FP&A
Company: Vandelay Industries
Location: Chicago, IL
Work model: Hybrid
Pay: $150,000 - $170,000 a year
Source: linkedin_email

Vandelay Industries imports and distributes industrial packaging and specialty plastics to manufacturers across North America. We run twelve distribution centers and employ about 1,800 people, with revenue of $900 million.

We are hiring a Director of FP&A to lead planning across our regional businesses. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and lead a team of four analysts.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual plan and the monthly forecast for the company and each region.
- Build the long-range plan and the business cases behind new distribution centers.
- Prepare the monthly results package for the executive team.
- Work in person with each regional general manager on their plan, their results and their cost structure.
- Lead and develop the FP&A team.

Travel: this role travels 30 to 40 percent of the time. You will spend at least one week a month at our distribution centers and regional offices, and more during planning season.

Qualifications:
- 8+ years in FP&A, including team leadership.
- Experience in distribution, logistics or manufacturing.
- Strong modeling skills and comfort working with operators on the floor.
- Willingness to travel as described above.

The role is hybrid, based at our Chicago headquarters when you are not traveling. The base salary range is $150,000 to $170,000, plus an annual bonus.

Vandelay Industries is an equal opportunity employer.
