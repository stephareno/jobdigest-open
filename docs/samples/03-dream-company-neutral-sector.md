# Director of Financial Planning
Company: Wayne Enterprises
Location: Chicago, IL
Work model: Hybrid

Wayne Enterprises is a family-controlled holding company. Its businesses span commercial real estate, property management and professional services. The group employs about 6,000 people, and its Chicago office runs finance for the holding company and three of its operating businesses.

We are hiring a Director of Financial Planning to lead planning for the Chicago group. You will report to the Vice President of Corporate Development and lead a team of five analysts.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual plan and the quarterly forecast for the holding company and its Chicago businesses.
- Build the long-range plan and the capital allocation analysis behind our investment decisions.
- Build the deal and portfolio models the corporate development team works from.
- Work with each operating business's finance lead on targets, results and variance explanations.
- Support the treasury team on cash and debt planning.
- Lead and develop the planning team.

Qualifications:
- 10+ years in FP&A, corporate finance or investment analysis, including team leadership.
- Experience with a multi-business or holding company structure.
- Clear writing and the judgment to know which number matters.
- MBA or CFA preferred.

The role is hybrid, with three days a week in our Chicago office on Wacker Drive. Compensation is competitive and includes an annual bonus.

Wayne Enterprises is an equal opportunity employer.
