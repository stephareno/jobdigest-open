# Director of FP&A
Company: Dunder Mifflin
Location: Remote (US)
Work model: Remote
Pay: $95,000 - $110,000 a year

Dunder Mifflin is a regional paper and office supplies distributor serving small and mid-size businesses across the Northeast and Midwest. We run eleven branches with about 900 employees and annual revenue of $310 million.

We are hiring a Director of FP&A to lead planning for the company. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer and manage two analysts: the team is two people today, with no plans to grow it.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual budget and the quarterly forecast for the company and each branch.
- Build branch profitability reporting and explain what drives each branch's results.
- Maintain the monthly branch scorecards the branch managers use.
- Support pricing analysis and customer profitability work with the sales team.
- Work with the controller on the monthly close and the variance commentary.
- Coach and develop the two analysts.

Qualifications:
- 7+ years in FP&A or corporate finance.
- Experience in distribution or another branch-based business.
- Advanced spreadsheet modeling skills and clear writing.
- Experience managing at least one analyst.

Our culture: our branches are close-knit, our customers stay with us for decades, and we value people who show up for each other.

This role is fully remote within the United States, with occasional visits to branches. The base salary range is $95,000 to $110,000, plus an annual bonus.

Dunder Mifflin is an equal opportunity employer.
