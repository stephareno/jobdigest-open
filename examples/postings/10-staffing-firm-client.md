# Director of FP&A (direct hire for Aperture Science)
Company: Hooli Talent Partners
Location: Remote (US)
Work model: Remote
Pay: $155,000 - $180,000 a year
Source: jsearch

Hooli Talent Partners is recruiting a Director of FP&A on behalf of our client, Aperture Science, a privately held research company that develops portable energy and materials technologies for industrial and government customers. Aperture Science employs about 1,100 people across two research campuses. This is a direct-hire role; you will be an Aperture Science employee.

The Director of FP&A reports to Aperture Science's Chief Financial Officer and leads a team of five.

Responsibilities:
- Own the annual plan and the quarterly forecast for the company's research programs and its commercial business.
- Build the long-range plan and the funding model behind each research program.
- Work with program managers on staffing plans and spend.
- Track program budgets against milestones with research leaders, and explain the variances.
- Support the pricing of government contracts and commercial agreements.
- Lead and develop the FP&A team.

Qualifications:
- 10+ years in FP&A, including leadership of a team.
- Experience at a research-driven company, in defense, energy or life sciences, or with government contracts.
- Strong modeling and communication skills.

Our client values rigor, curiosity and plain talk about results.

The role is remote within the United States, with quarterly visits to Aperture Science's research campus. The base salary range is $155,000 to $180,000, plus an annual bonus.

Hooli Talent Partners and Aperture Science are equal opportunity employers.
